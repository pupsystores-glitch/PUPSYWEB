Description

This harness is all about giving your dog that just-right fit with the comfort they deserve. Padded with breathable fabric and adjustable in four places, it wraps gently without restricting movement. The Y-shape keeps pressure off the neck, and the gold hardware adds a little glam to every walk.

Please note: Some product photos may still show our previous transparent buckles. Our latest production features upgraded color-matching buckles, along with slightly refined tones - so minor color differences may occur compared to past versions or example photos.
